Transformer-Based Multi-Horizon Electricity Demand Forecasting

The challenge: LSTM models for energy forecasting were bloated and plateauing in accuracy.

The approach: Engineered a custom Transformer architecture with multi-head self-attention and positional encodings. Processed 139,632+ half-hourly observations spanning meteorological, temporal, and grid interconnector variables. Benchmarked across nine forecast horizons from 1 hour to 4 weeks.

The result: 41% MAE improvement over LSTM baselines with 54% fewer parameters. Paper accepted at Springer ETMCIS 2026.
